Bloody Rally Show is a top-down combat racer set on a dystopian reality TV show. Drift, boost, ram and shoot your way to the front, keep the viewers entertained, and try to still have a car at the end of it.

[b]Race your friends online[/b]
Host a Custom Race, invite your Steam friends and settle things on the track. Online is friends-only: there are no public lobbies or matchmaking, you play with your Steam friends.
[list]
[*] Race, or fight it out in Car Battle, with AI racers to fill the grid
[*] Everyone picks their own car, paint job and upgrades
[*] Add weapons, missions, the director's car and helicopter fire
[*] Race on foot, which is exactly as bad an idea as it sounds
[*] The host's custom tracks and Workshop cars come along automatically, so nobody has to subscribe to anything
[*] Runs through Steam, with no ports to forward and no servers to rent
[/list]

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/BRSSteamDrift.gif[/img]

[b]Racing[/b]
Physics-based handling in the sweet spot between arcade and sim: easy to pick up, difficult to master. Learn how your car behaves on different surfaces, feel when it loses grip, and catch it before the wall does.
[list]
[*] An endless supply of randomly generated tracks, plus handcrafted ones
[*] Nitro boost that rewards picking your moment
[*] Ramps, jumps, puddles, oil spills, ice patches and explosive barrels
[*] Props that move, break and get in the way
[*] Daily Challenge and Time Attack against other players' ghosts
[*] A plethora of Leaderboards, where skill is the only way to the top
[/list]

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/BRSSteamFight1.gif[/img]

[b]Car battles[/b]
Use weapons and your car's inertia to frag everyone else while a battle royale-style shrinking area closes in. Or add Weapon Zones to a regular race, when a clean win feels too easy.

[b]Campaign[/b]
Procedurally generated campaigns with a dystopian story, missions and challenges. Earn XP, level up your racer, and tune and upgrade your car to keep your paymasters happy.
[list]
[*] Play solo, in local co-op, or over Steam Remote Play Together
[*] Names of top Leaderboard players show up as AI racers
[*] Enter a race on foot in Pedestrian Mode, take photos of passing cars for cash, and try to afford the medical bills
[/list]

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/BRSSteamCreate.gif[/img]

[b]Build your own[/b]
Create tracks and cars with the same in-game editors that were used to make all the content in the game. Share your creations on Steam Workshop, and download what other people have built.

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/BRSSteamCustomize.gif[/img]

[b]Make it yours[/b]
Use your own images for racer avatars and car paint jobs, add layers of details to your paint job, and change the names of your opponents in campaign or Custom Race. Name them after people you have a problem with.

[img]{STEAM_APP_IMAGE}/extras/BRSConsole.gif[/img]

[b]A built-in operating system[/b]
OK, OK, it's just a console. But you can run experiments that adjust the physics, change the game logic and do other cool stuff.

[b]Also included[/b]
[list]
[*] Split screen local multiplayer for up to 4 players
[*] Offline races with up to 100 cars
[*] AI racers of every skill level, who do stupid things from time to time
[*] Nearly 50 achievements
[*] High quality OST with a dozen full-length tunes
[*] A setting to turn off the blood, for family-friendly split screen sessions
[*] References to gluten free spelta bread and other bad jokes
[*] DRM-free! (Online play, Leaderboards and Workshop do need Steam)
[/list]
